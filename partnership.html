About

Online and behind the lens, most people know me as BombayMalayali. I was born in Kerala, grew up in Oman, spent nearly fourteen years in Mumbai, and now work between Amsterdam and Mumbai.

Photography has taken me across 25 countries. When ground level doesn’t do a place justice, I take to the air. My photographs have been exhibited on screens and in galleries in Times Square in New York, Tokyo, Rome, Dubai and Bangalore.

Certified drone pilot: EASA A1/A3 and A2. DGCA registered.
